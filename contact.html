Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this for my classroom? +

Absolutely! MindGrace Trivia is designed for educational use. Feel free to incorporate it into your curriculum or training programs.

How can I contribute questions? +

We welcome contributions from mental health professionals and educators. Send us your questions with proper DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 references.

Is this updated regularly? +

Yes! We periodically update the question bank to reflect changes in diagnostic criteria and add new categories.

Can I host this on my own server? +

Yes, as long as it's used for educational purposes. The entire application is client-side and can be hosted anywhere.
