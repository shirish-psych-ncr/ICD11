❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this platform really free?
Yes! MindGrace Trivia is completely free with no paywalls, subscriptions, or hidden costs. Our mission is to make psychiatric education accessible to everyone.
What classification systems are covered?
We cover both DSM-5-TR (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, 5th Edition, Text Revision) and ICD-11 (International Classification of Diseases, 11th Revision).
Can I use this for clinical practice?
No. This platform is strictly for educational purposes. Always consult official diagnostic manuals and qualified healthcare professionals for clinical decision-making.
How often are new questions added?
We regularly update our question bank. Check back frequently for new content across different diagnostic categories.
