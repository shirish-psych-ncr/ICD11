Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Mind Grace Neuropsychiatric Team ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our MindGrace Trivia educational platform.

Key Point: MindGrace Trivia is a client-side application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from users.

Information Collection

No Personal Data Collected

MindGrace Trivia operates entirely in your browser. We do not:

  • Collect personal information (name, email, address)
  • Track your browsing behavior
  • Use cookies or local storage for tracking
  • Transmit data to external servers during gameplay

Game Statistics

Your game scores and progress are stored temporarily in your browser's memory only for the duration of your session. Once you close the browser tab, this information is cleared automatically.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Google Fonts for typography. When you load the page, your browser may connect to Google's servers to download font files. This is standard practice for modern websites.

We do not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Security

Since we don't collect or store user data, there is no risk of data breaches or unauthorized access to your personal information through our platform.

The application code is open and transparent - you can inspect all functionality through your browser's developer tools.

Educational Use Disclaimer

MindGrace Trivia is designed for educational purposes only. The diagnostic codes and clinical information provided are for learning purposes and should not be used for actual clinical decision-making.

We make no claims about the completeness or accuracy of the diagnostic information. Always refer to official DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 documentation published by the American Psychiatric Association and World Health Organization.

Children's Privacy

Our educational content is suitable for students and professionals studying mental health. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, though our site does not collect any user data whatsoever.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
