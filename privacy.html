📊 Data Collection

MindGrace Trivia is a client-side application. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All progress data (scores, statistics) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device.

🍪 Cookies

We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies. The only data stored is in localStorage for maintaining your session statistics.

🔒 Data Security

Since no data is transmitted to our servers, there is no risk of data breaches from our end. Your learning progress remains entirely on your device.

📱 Third-Party Services

This site uses Google Fonts for typography. Google may collect usage data according to their privacy policy. No personal information is intentionally shared.

👤 Your Rights

You can clear all stored data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or using private/incognito browsing mode.
