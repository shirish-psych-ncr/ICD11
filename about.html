About MindGrace Trivia

Created by Shirish | Mind Grace Neuropsychiatric Team

MindGrace Trivia is an educational platform designed to help students and professionals master psychiatric diagnostic coding using both DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 classification systems.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide free, high-quality educational resources for learning psychiatric diagnostics without paywalls or barriers. We believe knowledge should be accessible to all learners.

✨ Key Features

  • Dual classification system training (DSM-5-TR & ICD-11)
  • Clinical vignettes with realistic patient scenarios
  • Immediate feedback with detailed explanations
  • Clinical pearls for enhanced learning
  • Progress tracking and session reports
  • Completely free - no subscriptions or paywalls

⚠️ Disclaimer

This platform is for educational purposes only. It is not intended for clinical decision-making or diagnosis. Always consult official diagnostic manuals and qualified healthcare professionals for clinical work.
