About MindGrace Trivia

MindGrace Trivia is an interactive educational platform designed to help mental health professionals, students, and enthusiasts deepen their understanding of psychiatric diagnoses through gamified learning.

🧠
Created by Shirish
Mind Grace Neuropsychiatric Team

Our mission is to make learning about mental health classification systems engaging, accessible, and effective. By combining the DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 frameworks into a single interactive experience, we bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application.

What Makes This Different

  • 📚
    Dual Classification System

    Every question displays both DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 codes for comprehensive learning.

  • 🎯
    Category Filtering

    Focus on specific diagnostic categories that matter most to your studies.

  • 💡
    Instant Feedback

    Learn from every answer with detailed explanations and code references.

  • 🔓
    Completely Free

    No paywalls, no subscriptions. Educational resource for everyone.

  • 📱
    Works Everywhere

    Pure HTML/CSS/JS - runs on any device with a modern browser.

Educational Purpose

This tool is designed for educational purposes only. It is not intended to be used as a clinical or diagnostic tool. Always refer to official DSM-5-TR and ICD-11 documentation published by the American Psychiatric Association and World Health Organization respectively for clinical decision-making.

The questions and content are regularly updated to reflect the latest revisions in both classification systems.

Contact & Contributions

We welcome feedback, suggestions for new questions, and corrections. If you're an educator or mental health professional interested in contributing to this resource, please reach out.
